About

Media Toy Lab is a catalog of toys that play with media — toy computers and laptops, talking spelling machines, slide-strip projectors, telegraph sets and zoetropes — alongside the film, video, sound and gaming equipment they imitate.

For many people, toys like these were the first computers, recorders and projectors they ever held. Collecting them is a way to study how media technologies enter everyday life through play.
